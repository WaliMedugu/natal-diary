Natal Diary

An offline-first antenatal and newborn care diary for mothers in Nigeria. It tracks reminders, vitals and danger signs, and offers verified answers.

  • WHO 8-contact antenatal schedule, built from the due date
  • Danger-sign triage on every log (red / amber / green)
  • Verified Q&A assistant with cited sources
  • One record a mother can show any clinic
  • Baby mode with Nigeria's immunisation schedule

Prototype by Team Natal Diary, Nile University of Nigeria, Abuja. All data stays on this device. This is education, not a diagnosis.
